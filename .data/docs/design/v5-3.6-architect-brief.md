## Rules

- **You bring decided research together; you don't invent mechanisms.** Where research leaves a choice open, decide
  it by reasoning and consistency with `v5.md` §1, in design-first form: the problem with its hard constraint, the
  2–3 real options with trade-offs, the choice and why.
- **Resolve every conflict (§2), simplification (§3) and question (§4) that touches what you own.** Record the
  outcome of each by its id.
- **No simplifications, in wording or mechanism.** Lync never acts on input beyond dropping malformed input and
  reporting facts: no clamping, rewriting, throttling or catch-all policies (checks' `policy = clamp` goes). No
  presets, tiers, merged modes, or one knob covering several triggers. Every default derived with its inputs shown,
  or marked "chosen" with what would change it. No vague quantities or judging words.
- **Precise.** Every option: name, type (integer or real), unit, range, default, cost. Every fact: code, exact
  trigger, evidence fields with units, known causes on an unmodified client (or "none" with the argument).
- **Nothing is deferred to a later milestone.** Items the research placed in milestone 7 are specified now.
- **Facts, not verdicts.** R22 (quiet mitigation hooks), R23 (suspicion scores) and R24 (automatic consequences)
  stay refused by this principle; the integrity unit records them.
- **Performance is a cost to state, never a veto on its own.** Security comes first.
- **Lean.** Cut what doesn't change what gets built. One way to do each thing.
- **Grounded.** Where a Luau or Roblox fact decides a question, check it with the `anthropic-skills:luau` skill or
  cite the brief that did; otherwise mark it unverified.

## How to write

- Revise your files in place, one section per edit. Put "draft 3.6, 2026-10-15" at the top of each file you own
  (for `v5.md`, only the core-api unit edits the header).
- Change record: `design/v5-3.6-changes-<unit>.md`. Start it with a checklist of the sections you will change and
  tick them as you go. Then, for every §1 entry, conflict, simplification and question you handled: its id, the
  outcome (accepted, changed or rejected) and the reason. Then decisions you made yourself. Then "Needs elsewhere".
- If an edit fails, mark it "not done" in the checklist and continue.
- End with a consistency pass over what you own: section references, examples, tables.
- Don't commit.
- Return only: the paths, counts by outcome, and at most eight lines on the most significant changes.

## Decisions made in this unit

1. **The caller bit travels in the header and the lane byte** (§4.5 decision): one hash per candidate.
2. **Budget-refused reliable frames are not hashed and advance e by 1** (§4.5): the per-player tag CPU is bounded
   by the budgets, formula and figures stated.
3. **`REKEY` switches the server at send; no `REKEY-ACK`**: control type 4 unused (rule 7).
4. **`desync` counts reliable frames only**: `REKEY` repairs the reliable count, and the 13.3/s bound needs it.
5. **Derivation inputs**: nonce ‖ `01` for the key, ‖ `02` for the permutation seed; the layout seed over
   permutation seed ‖ `layout`. Key nonce per connection, permutation nonce per server.
6. **Protocol and serialization versions stay 1**: they count released forms, and none is released.
7. **`layout` per `WELCOME`, and a generated view refuses `layout` 1 natively.**
8. **`delay` in the first `WELCOME` (D₀, integer formula) and in `RATE`.**
9. **The permutation seed stays 32 bits** (RR3 R2's search figure stated).
10. **No downstream or upstream `strings` text in §4** until its tables are designed (entry 34).
11. **Check-then-write only in generated encoders without `t.convert`/`t.custom`.**
12. **Golden set regenerated** by `probes/design-3.6/golden_wire.luau`; G4c, DERIVE, W1, W2 added; changed vectors
    marked unverified until a milestone build reproduces them.
13. **Forced control-frame sizes recomputed** for the 3.6 header (+1 B over RR2/CF's figures).

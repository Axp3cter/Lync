## Decisions made here

1. **The client's allowance stays in elements.** Problem: the server counts decode units, the client 2^22 elements.
   Units price one player's cost to a shared server; a client decodes only its game's server and its allowance only
   stops an unbounded count, and unit prices on devices are unmeasured. Kept in elements (§7.5 bullet; §4.9 agrees).
2. **The stream copy term lowers the units default.** For the 2 ms/s target to hold with streams, the
   `inbound.decodeUnits` default subtracts C_s: 10,000 units/s with no stream, 7,000 with one 42-bit stream. The
   alternative (a bound term outside the target) would let a schema with streams exceed the chosen target.
3. **Step 5 order.** Answers, resolution and reachability before `limit` (they see over-limit messages, as before);
   traps after `limit` (integrity's decision); report-only checks last, never on a trap's message.
4. **String tables are milestone 2** (§10): §4.11 belonged to no milestone; it is §4 wire beside the frames, so its
   declaration codes are milestone 1–2 codes (77 in all).

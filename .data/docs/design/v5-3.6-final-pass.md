- [x] **The refused-call walk.** Problem: a call refused at the byte bucket took no tokens, so ~500 calls a second
  could each re-walk tokens / 8 entries (~16 ms/s per lane outside the bound). Options: charge the walk, or cap it

  walk; a walk that passes the tokens refuses the frame and leaves the bucket at 0. Every entry read is a byte paid,
  so the walk is inside c_b × B (§6.1 step 2, §7.3 `inboundBytesExceeded`, §7.5, §10 row).
